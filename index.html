BarrelBook Privacy Policy

What We Know About You

Spoiler alert: Nothing!

BarrelBook only stores what you tell it to:

  • Your whiskey collection (names, types, proof, prices, etc.)
  • Your tasting notes and ratings
  • Your wishlist items
  • Your infinity bottle data
  • Your app preferences

What we DON'T collect:

  • Your name, email, or personal info
  • Where you are
  • What you're doing on your phone
  • Any data that leaves your device
  • Any of your personal whiskey data

Your whiskey collection is your business. We can't see it, and neither can anyone else.

Your Data, Your Rules

You're in complete control:

  • Export your data anytime through the app
  • Delete everything by uninstalling the app
  • Your data lives on your device, so you call the shots
