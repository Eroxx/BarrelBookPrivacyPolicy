BarrelBook Privacy Policy

The short version: there is no BarrelBook server. Your collection lives on your devices and in your own iCloud account, and none of it ever reaches us. No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.

What BarrelBook stores, and where

Only what you enter: your bottles (names, types, proof, prices, dates, notes), tastings and ratings, wishlist and replacement bottles, infinity bottles, favorite stores, web reviews you save, and your app settings.

  • On your device, in the app's own storage.
  • In your private iCloud database, if you're signed in to iCloud, so your iPhone and iPad stay in sync. Apple holds it under your Apple Account; we cannot see or access it.
  • A short device list in your iCloud: for each of your devices with BarrelBook, its type (iPhone, iPad), model, system and app version, when it last opened and synced, and how many bottles and tastings it held. BarrelBook uses this to show you which devices have your collection and whether they're in sync. It stays in your iCloud account.

Permissions, and what each is for

  • Location, only if you allow it: used on your device to find liquor stores near you through Apple Maps. Apple receives the search as with any Maps search. BarrelBook does not store or send your location anywhere else.
  • Camera, only if you allow it: the label scanner reads a bottle's label on your device. No photo is saved, added to your library, or sent anywhere.

Other services

  • Apple: the App Store handles purchases; iCloud handles sync. Both are covered by Apple's privacy policy.
  • Web pages you open: looking up reviews opens pages (such as a Google or Whiskybase search) in an in-app browser. Those sites see the visit as they would from any browser. Reviews you save are kept with your collection.
  • Sharing and export: when you share your wishlist or export a CSV, you choose where it goes.

BarrelBook contains no advertising, analytics, or tracking code, and nothing is sold or shared.

Deleting your data

  • In the app, Settings › Delete Data removes your data from every device signed in to your iCloud account.
  • Deleting the app removes the copy on that device. The iCloud copy remains until you delete it from iCloud: Settings › your name › iCloud › Manage Account Storage › BarrelBook.
  • You can export everything to a CSV file at any time.
